YStreamer is software for a **Raspberry Pi 4** that takes live video from DJI
FPV goggles, or from a USB camera, and sends it to a screen, to browsers, to
streams and to recordings, all at the same time. You set it up from a web
interface on the device.

It's free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.

## Inputs

| Source                                        | Port  | Notes                                            |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| DJI Goggles 2, Integra, Goggles 3, N3         | USB-C | Video, camera settings, battery and link quality |
| DJI FPV Goggles V1 / V2                       | USB-A | Video only. Experimental                         |
| USB cameras, capture sticks, analog receivers | USB-A | Encoded to H.264 on the Pi                       |

One source is used at a time; you pick it under **Input**.

## Outputs

All of these can run at once:

- **HDMI**: the Pi's HDMI port, to a TV, monitor or projector.
- **Browser**: live view in the web interface, on any phone or computer that
  can reach the device.
- **RTSP**: a server that players like VLC and OBS connect to.
- **SRT**: waits for a receiver, or sends to one.
- **RTMP**: sends to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook or any RTMP server.
- **UDP**: sends RTP or MPEG-TS to up to 8 receivers, such as QGroundControl
  or Mission Planner.
- **Recording**: MP4 or MPEG-TS files on the SD card or a USB drive.

## Also

- The air unit's camera settings from the browser: exposure, white balance,
  video format, colour profile and more.
- The goggles' battery and the link quality, next to the video.
- A logo on the HDMI picture, and a splash screen while there's no video.
- A physical record button, with an optional light.
- Remote access through Tailscale or WireGuard.
- Signed updates from the web interface, with rollback.
