Under **Network → Remote access**. The ready-made image comes with Tailscale
and WireGuard installed. After the install script, the card says what to
install if they're missing. Both need the device to have internet:
through your network, Ethernet, or a [phone over USB](/docs/network/wifi-and-ethernet).

<Admonition type="warning" title="Set a password first">
  Without a [password](/docs/network/security), anyone on your Tailscale network
  or VPN can change the settings.
</Admonition>

## Tailscale

1. Press **Set up**. The card shows a link.
2. Open it on any device and sign in to Tailscale. The card follows along by
   itself.
3. Once connected, the card shows the device's Tailscale address. Open it from
   any device on your Tailscale network.

The card also shows the devices using it, and whether each connection is
**Direct** or **Relayed**. Relayed adds delay.

**Disconnect** takes the device off Tailscale for now; **Sign out** removes it
from your Tailscale network. Setting it up again needs a connection from the
local network.

## WireGuard

For your own WireGuard server.

1. Make a configuration for the device on your server: a `.conf` file with an
   `[Interface]` and a `[Peer]`.
2. Press **Upload configuration** and choose the file.
3. **Switch on**.

The card shows the device's address in the tunnel, the last answer from the
server, and the data received and sent.

<Admonition type="tip" title="PersistentKeepalive">
  The device usually sits behind a router, so the server can only reach it while
  the line is kept open. Add `PersistentKeepalive = 25` under `[Peer]`; the card
  warns when it's missing.
</Admonition>

A configuration with `AllowedIPs = 0.0.0.0/0` sends all of the device's
internet traffic through the tunnel, including RTMP and SRT streams.

**Remove** deletes the configuration and its keys from the device.
