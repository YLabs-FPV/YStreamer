## Performance

The **Performance** page charts the last half hour, recorded every second:
processor load and clock, memory, temperature, the video's frame rate and
bitrate, network traffic, viewers and outputs, and the Wi-Fi signal.

It warns when the power supply dipped below what the Pi needs. That causes
glitches and USB drop-outs: use a stronger supply or a shorter cable.

## Logs

The **Logs** page shows the latest lines from the YStreamer service. With
**Auto-refresh** on (the default), new lines appear every second, and the page
keeps to the newest unless you've scrolled up to read. The choice is
remembered in that browser. They're
the first place to look when something doesn't work, and worth including when
you report a problem.

Over SSH, `ystreamer logs` shows them too, and `ystreamer logs -f` keeps
following. See [the command line](/docs/maintenance/command-line).

## About

**System → About** shows the board, the system and kernel, the YStreamer
version, the serial number, uptime, processor temperature and power. Its
**Report an issue** link opens a new issue on GitHub.

## Restarting

Under **System → Service**:

- **Restart YStreamer**: restarts the service. Video stops for a few seconds.
- **Restart USB link**: makes the goggles connect again, as if the cable had
  been replugged. Try it first when the goggles are plugged in but there's no
  video.

Under **System → General → Power**: **Reboot device** and **Power off
device**. Power off before unplugging, especially while recording.

## Developer mode

For verbose logging, a terminal on the device and tools for looking into the
link to the goggles, turn on [developer mode](/docs/maintenance/developer-mode).
