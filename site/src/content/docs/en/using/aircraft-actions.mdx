With **DJI goggles** as the source, the Watch page can show the buttons DJI
Fly offers for the aircraft: take off, land, cancel landing, return to home
(RTH) and cancel RTH. YStreamer sends the same command DJI Fly sends for each
of them, through the goggles to the aircraft.

It's off until you turn it on, and meant for an aircraft that's flown by
someone holding its remote.

<Admonition type="warning" title="You stay the pilot">
  These buttons don't replace the remote. Keep it in hand, switched on and ready
  to take over: a command from the browser can arrive late, or not at all, and
  the aircraft does what it's told even when that's a bad idea, such as taking
  off indoors or landing on water. You're responsible for the flight and for
  following the rules where you fly.
</Admonition>

## Turning it on

1. Set a [password](/docs/network/security#web-interface-password) under **System → General →
   Access**. Without one, everyone who can open the web interface counts as
   an admin, so the switch stays off.
2. Under **Input**, with **DJI goggles** as the source, turn on **Aircraft
   actions** and confirm the warning.
3. Save.

The **actions** panel then shows on the Watch page, beside the video, for
admins only. People who are only watching never see it, and the device
refuses the commands from them.

## Using the buttons

| Button         | What the aircraft does                                  |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Take off       | Starts the motors and climbs to a hover                 |
| Land           | Descends and lands where it is                          |
| Return to home | Flies back to its home point and lands there            |
| Cancel RTH     | Stops returning home and hovers                         |
| Cancel landing | Stops an automatic landing, including the end of an RTH |

Each button has to be **held for about a second**: a bar fills up while you
hold, and letting go early cancels. One hold sends one command. Holding on
doesn't send it again.

The buttons only work once the goggles link is live and the aircraft is
linked to it. Until then the panel says what it's waiting for, and the
device refuses the command.

After each press, the panel says whether the aircraft answered. If it didn't,
YStreamer sends the same command again, as DJI Fly does, at most four times in
two seconds, then stops. No answer doesn't mean nothing happened: watch the
aircraft and be ready on the remote.

<Admonition type="info" title="Return to home needs a home point">
  The aircraft records its home point once it has a good GPS fix, as it does
  with the remote alone. Without one it can't return home. YStreamer doesn't
  check this itself, so look at the goggles before you rely on RTH.
</Admonition>

## The log

Every press is written to the [logs](/docs/maintenance/diagnostics) with the
time, the action, and whether the aircraft answered or the device refused it
and why. The lines start with `[aircraft]`.

## Tested with

Return to home, cancel RTH and cancel landing are byte for byte what DJI Fly
sent in recordings of it flying our own DJI Neo 2. All five commands are also
in the public DJI protocol notes of
[dji-firmware-tools](https://github.com/o-gs/dji-firmware-tools). Take off and
land have been flown through DJI Goggles 3 with exactly these commands, from
another device on the same goggles link. From YStreamer itself, and RTH from
anything but DJI Fly, they're still untested. If you try them,
[tell us how it went](https://github.com/YLabs-FPV/YStreamer/issues/new?template=hardware_report.yml).
