## Splash screen

While there's no video, YStreamer sends a splash screen instead: on HDMI, to
browsers and to the streams. Recordings don't get it.

By default it shows how to reach the device: its addresses, the access
point's name and password, and a QR code to join it.

Under **Display → Splash screen**:

| Setting                              | What it does                                                           |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Upload image                         | Your own picture instead of the default. **Use default** goes back.    |
| Image scaling                        | **Fit** or **Fill**, for your image.                                   |
| Show status over custom image        | Keeps the addresses and connection status along the bottom.            |
| Show access point password on screen | Off hides the password and the QR code.                                |
| Freeze on last frame                 | When the video stops, keep the last picture instead.                   |
| Grayscale frozen frame               | Shows the frozen picture in grey, so it can't be taken for live video. |

<Admonition type="tip">
  In a public place, turn off **Show access point password on screen**, or
  change the password under **Network**.
</Admonition>

## Logo

Under **Display → Logo**, drop in an image: PNG, WebP or JPEG, with
transparency kept. Then drag it where you want it on the preview, and drag its
corner to resize it, or use the **Size** slider. **Opacity** goes from 10 to
100%, and **Show logo** turns it on and off without removing it.

Where the logo appears depends on the source:

- **DJI goggles**: on **HDMI** only, and in the browser view. The streams and
  recordings carry the goggles' own video, untouched.
- **USB camera**: in the video itself, so on every output and in recordings.
